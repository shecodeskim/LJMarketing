About L&J

Nairobi's Strategy-First Marketing Agency

"We believe every Kenyan business deserves marketing that actually works."

L&J Marketing Kenya is a results-driven marketing consultancy and agency based in Upperhill, Nairobi. Founded by a BCom Marketing Management graduate with hands-on agency experience, we serve SMEs, startups, real estate firms, schools, clinics, and service businesses across Kenya and East Africa.

We are not a "post and pray" agency. Every campaign begins with a thorough understanding of your business, your market, and your competition — then we build a strategy designed to convert attention into revenue.
